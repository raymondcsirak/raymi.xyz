Raymond Csirák: Uptime

Works, 2008 to the present

This exhibition follows the working life of Raymond Csirák, Senior Site Reliability Engineer, from a service desk in Sighetu Marmației in 2008 to private cloud at GoDaddy. Most of the works on display were built to go unnoticed. When they did their job, nobody looked at them. Here, for once, they are lit properly.

I

Room I / 2008 to 2018

The Workshop Years

The early works were made for whoever called. At Ltt Halo in Sighetu Marmației, Csirák supported business and home clients and built the networks they ran on. In 2015 he joined noLimits Technologies in Satu Mare, where he moved from first-line support and technical sales to managing the helpdesk team.

Room II Infrastructure

II

Room II / 2019 to 2024

Infrastructure

Head of Infrastructure and Linux Systems Administrator, noLimits Technologies and Hexalab SRL, Satu Mare. January 2019 to March 2024.

In 2019 Csirák took charge of the infrastructure itself. The work grew larger and quieter: virtualization platforms, Linux and network estates, Level 3 support for business clients, supplier contracts and project plans. Visitors may notice that nothing in this room appears to be broken. That was the point.

Room III Platforms

III

Room III / 2022 to 2026

Platforms

DevOps and Platform Engineer, Hexalab SRL (formerly Hitter Technologies), Satu Mare. March 2022 to May 2026.

At Hexalab, Csirák turned infrastructure into platforms. Kubernetes clusters were described in code and run mostly on bare metal. Releases travelled through GitLab pipelines into Argo CD. Backups were made to be restored. He also led the team's adoption of AI tools, with demos and clear rules for using them safely.

Room IV Scale

IV

Room IV / 2026 onward

Scale

Senior Site Reliability Engineer, GoDaddy. Remote, Romania. May 2026 to present.

The newest room is still being installed. Csirák now works on private cloud at GoDaddy: large OpenStack deployments, automated with Puppet and Ansible and supported by tooling he writes in Python. The work is ongoing and may be viewed in progress.
