The reading experience

A blog post needs room for more than a headline. Sometimes a short explanation is enough. Sometimes the useful part is a command, a diagram, or the detail that made a problem finally make sense.

This page is here to test that space. The type, colours, and spacing follow the original portfolio. The line length stays comfortable on a wide screen, and the layout becomes a single column on a phone.

The useful detail is often the one that did not fit in the summary.
